"Let's celebrate events", curated by "The Catering Inc."…

Celebrations infuse life with passion and purpose but at "The Catering Inc.", we handcraft them with love. We understand how important this day is for you and we wish to put forward a memory you would cherish for a lifetime. An event your guests would remember and narrate its stories for times to come. "The Catering Inc." takes you through a gastronomical and artistic journey, vibes of which will linger with you.

Be it a small function or the D-Day, "The Catering Inc." team is here to handle all diverse events with non-repetitive taste and presentation of food. We create the most plausible fine dine experience, because you deserve nothing less on the most important day of your life. We provide 360 degree holistic service where we take care of your food, its plating, Food | Décor, Service, Crockery, Cutlery, right temperature at which food is served and presented, right condiments to enhance the flavor of dishes and things that matter. From menu designing to keeping it up with latest food trends to making sure we take care of guests with allergens and specific dietary requirements… We do it all…

"The Catering Inc." is a company managed and run by "Chef Gautam Chaudhry". Chef Gautam is well recognized for his bold flavors and progressive cuisine concepts and carries more than 2 decades of experience. During his working tenure, he has served some top hotels like Oberoi's, Hyatt, Radisson, etc at various leadership positions. There are a number of awards & accolades that he carries in this arena.

We have curated events all over the world, from destination weddings to luxury & ultra-luxury weddings. We take pride in our customer service & quality of food we serve. The progressive & tailor made menu for each function ensures that you do not get run of the mill food, because to us you are extremely unique and we would love to make your occasion stand out from peers.
